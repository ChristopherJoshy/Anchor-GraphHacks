ANCHOR PROJECT RULES
====================

PRODUCT
-------
1. Product name: Anchor.
2. Track: Track 01 — Agents That Act on Connected Data.
3. Core differentiator: graph-backed recovery evaluated against bounded follow-on failure counterexamples.
4. The stress suite is finite and declared. Passing means only that these cases passed; it is not formal proof or a guarantee of resilience.
5. Execution is simulation-only. No live purchasing, payments, production infrastructure changes, or external operational writes.

DATA AND CLAIMS
---------------
6. Use only synthetic, public, or team-owned data that may lawfully be used. Demo suppliers, orders, contracts, capacities, and commercial dependencies are synthetic.
7. Label observed/verified facts, imported facts, synthetic facts, assumptions, inferences, and simulated outcomes distinctly.
8. Never invent suppliers, capacities, inventory, prices, route times, approvals, or receipts.
9. Never claim a feature, integration, test, benchmark, participant count, award, or deployment unless directly verified. No unmeasured performance claims.
10. Do not claim real-time global visibility, academic ESRI equivalence, universal resilience, or formal verification.

GRAPH AND AGENT SAFETY
---------------------
11. FalkorDB is the primary graph store and must perform essential dependency traversal, shared-upstream analysis, scenario/evidence retrieval, and persistent graph history.
12. If FalkorDB is unavailable, graph-dependent features fail clearly. Do not silently substitute an in-memory graph or LLM guesses.
13. Use fixed parameterized Cypher templates, typed schemas, and explicit allowlists. Never expose arbitrary Cypher, SQL, shell, or unrestricted file access as agent tools.
14. Treat imported text and model output as untrusted. Policy and hard feasibility constraints are enforced in code, not delegated to an LLM.
15. Every simulated write requires mode, policy, current-state/version, and idempotency checks; verify it by reading persisted mock ERP state.
16. Bound retries, scenario search, and replanning. On infeasibility, stale state, missing evidence, or non-convergence, report the condition and unresolved demand.

HACKATHON INTEGRITY
-------------------
17. Follow the official rules at https://www.wemakedevs.org/hackathons/falkordb/rules; they supersede this file.
18. Main coding/design work must be performed during Oct 15–18, 2026 IST. Properly licensed libraries and templates may be reused; do not copy another entry.
19. Disclose AI assistance. Team members must meaningfully review and understand the implementation and be able to explain graph modeling, queries, architecture, and decisions.
20. A public repository, setup instructions, graph model, key Cypher/algorithms, demo video, and selected track are required by the official rules. Do not claim readiness until each exists and is checked.
21. Do not spend money, publish, deploy to public infrastructure, or submit the entry without explicit team authorization.

DOCUMENTATION
-------------
22. Keep AGENTS.md, Idea.md, rules.txt, and docs/research.md, docs/product-decision.md, docs/architecture.md, docs/participant-explanation.md, docs/ai-disclosure.md consistent.
23. Cite direct sources and publication dates. Mark access dates for mutable documentation and GitHub pages. Separate vendor statements, entrant self-description, independent evidence, and our interpretation.
