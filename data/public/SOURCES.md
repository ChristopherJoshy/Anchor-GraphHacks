# Public data sources

Checked 18 Oct 2026. The application does not ingest public supplier, contract, inventory, route, or disruption datasets. Commercial records are synthetic and generated by `src/anchor/seed.py`.

The seed includes 15 public city reference points with WGS84 coordinates. Names and coordinates are copied from the GeoNames city records linked below. GeoNames makes its database available under CC BY 4.0 and requests attribution. The points are map references only; they do not identify any synthetic supplier's real facility.

Attribution: Geographic names and reference coordinates from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/), CC BY 4.0, accessed 18 Oct 2026. GeoNames describes its coordinates as WGS84 in its [about page](https://www.geonames.org/about.html); its data/download page lists the CC BY attribution terms at [GeoNames export](https://www.geonames.org/export/).
