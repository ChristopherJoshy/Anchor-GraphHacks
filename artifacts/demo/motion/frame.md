Anime-inspired information design without characters. Thick ink contours, offset solid cel shadows, diagonal panel cuts, oversized condensed lettering, halftone print texture, drawn paths, anticipatory squash and pop, and directional speed accents. The dependency itself is the antagonist; the viewer follows a clean decision chain.

Headlines 110–190 px. Body 34–46 px. Evidence labels at least 25 px. Keep stable text holds after energetic entrances. Stage only one major focal animation at a time. No strobing/full-screen flashes; transitions use continuous ink shutters and dot masks. Color communicates roles: coral failure, cyan graph evidence, yellow decisions, green read-back process; never fabricate a green successful run state.

All diagrams have an editorial label. Footage has a recorded-source label. All supplier/order/incident/cost context is synthetic. Actual evidence stays visually unmodified except crop, scale, source trimming, and explanatory framing.

## v03 layout and choreography

Explain cause and consequence: count the order and stock; fill 7 of 20 demand squares; expose 13 missing squares; interrupt moving supply tokens; trace B and C into the shared upstream target; build the recorded allocation; reduce the capacity bar; reveal E in the new mix; send action/state/read-back tokens through the verification chain.

Keep proof windows stationary. Their source labels sit above the border, explanatory cards sit below, and no dot transition enters or leaves a recording scene. The web clip preserves its full app frame and map attribution. Text remains stable after each reveal. Keep synthetic voice and attributed music credits visible at the close; optional subtitle tracks are off by default so they do not cover evidence.
